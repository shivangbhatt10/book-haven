Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about ordering, shipping, returns, and more.

What are your shipping options?

We offer free standard shipping on all orders over $50. For orders under $50, standard shipping is $4.99. Expedited shipping is available at checkout for an additional fee.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3-7 business days within the continental US. International shipping times vary by location but typically take 7-21 business days.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase for unused, undamaged books in their original condition. Please contact us to initiate a return.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes! At checkout, you can select gift wrapping for $2.99 per order. We'll include a personalized message if you provide one.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive an email with tracking information. You can also log into your account to view order status.

Do you sell eBooks?

Currently, we specialize in physical books only. However, we're exploring digital options for the future!

Can I suggest a book for your store?

Absolutely! We love reader recommendations. Please email suggestions to suggestions@bookhaven.com.
